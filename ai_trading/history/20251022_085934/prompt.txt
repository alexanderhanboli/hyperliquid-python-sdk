TAKE PROFIT & STOP LOSS GUIDELINES:
Follow professional trading practices to maximize profitability while managing risk effectively:

1. USE ATR-BASED DYNAMIC STOPS (RECOMMENDED APPROACH):
   ATR (Average True Range) measures market volatility and helps set intelligent stops/targets.
   You are provided with both 3-Period ATR (short-term) and 14-Period ATR (standard).
   
   Professional ATR-based formula:
   - STOP LOSS: Entry ± (1.5 to 2.0) × 14-Period ATR
   - TAKE PROFIT: Entry ± (3.0 to 4.0) × 14-Period ATR
   
   This approach:
   - Automatically adapts to market volatility (high volatility = wider stops, low volatility = tighter stops)
   - Typically results in 4-6% take profit targets for crypto markets
   - Maintains healthy 1:2 or better risk/reward ratios
   - Avoids premature stop-outs during normal price fluctuations

2. RISK/REWARD RATIO (MANDATORY):
   - MUST maintain at least 1:2 ratio (e.g., 2% stop loss → 4% take profit minimum)
   - Professional traders prefer 1:2.5 or 1:3 ratios when market conditions allow
   - Never enter a trade with worse than 1:2 risk/reward

3. TAKE PROFIT TARGETS:
   - Recommended range: 4% - 6% from entry price
   - This range is realistic for crypto markets in 3-minute intervals
   - Can be extended beyond 6% if ATR and market momentum justify it
   - Prioritize probability of profit over maximum profit

4. STOP LOSS SETTINGS:
   - Recommended range: 1.5% - 3% from entry price
   - Must provide enough room for normal price fluctuations (use ATR as guide)
   - Tighter stops (1.5-2%) for low volatility, wider stops (2-3%) for high volatility
   
5. PRACTICAL EXAMPLE (ATR-Based Trading):
   Scenario: BNB trading at $1000, 14-Period ATR = $20
   
   For a LONG position:
   - Stop Loss: $1000 - (2.0 × $20) = $960 (4% below entry, or 2.0 × ATR)
   - Take Profit: $1000 + (4.0 × $20) = $1080 (8% above entry, or 4.0 × ATR)
   - Risk/Reward: 4% risk for 8% profit = 1:2 ratio ✅
   
   For a SHORT position:
   - Stop Loss: $1000 + (2.0 × $20) = $1040 (4% above entry, or 2.0 × ATR)
   - Take Profit: $1000 - (4.0 × $20) = $920 (8% below entry, or 4.0 × ATR)
   - Risk/Reward: 4% risk for 8% profit = 1:2 ratio ✅

   - Take Profit: $1000 ± $40 (4% move, or 4.0 × ATR)
